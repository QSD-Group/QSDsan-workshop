# Optional pre-reading

Total: 35 minutes for the core list, up to 55 with the extras. Nothing here is required. Skim, do not study.

## Core (35 minutes)

| Time | Read | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| 20 min | The QSDsan paper: Li et al. (2022), *QSDsan: an integrated platform for quantitative sustainable design of sanitation and resource recovery systems*, Environ. Sci.: Water Res. Technol. 8, 2289-2303. [Open access](https://doi.org/10.1039/D2EW00455K) (DOI 10.1039/D2EW00455K) | Read the abstract, the introduction, the figure that shows the platform structure, and the headings of the two case studies. Skip the methods detail. |
| 10 min | [Tutorial 0, Quick Overview](https://qsdsan.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorials/0_Quick_Overview.html) | The objects QSDsan uses (components, streams, units, systems) and how a result looks. |
| 5 min | [Jupyter tips](https://qsdsan.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorials/jupyter_tips.html) | How to read documentation for any QSDsan object from inside a notebook (`?` and Shift+Tab). |

## Extras (up to 20 minutes)

| Time | Read | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 10 min | [Tutorial 6, System](https://qsdsan.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorials/6_System.html) | Session 1, part 1 builds a system. Skim the headings and the first examples. |
| 10 min | Headings and introductions of [Tutorial 7, TEA](https://qsdsan.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorials/7_TEA.html) and [Tutorial 8, LCA](https://qsdsan.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorials/8_LCA.html) | Session 1, part 2. Useful if you already know TEA or LCA and want to see how QSDsan sets up the calculation. |

## Before session 2 (optional, 10 minutes)

- Headings of [Tutorial 9, Uncertainty and Sensitivity Analyses](https://qsdsan.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorials/9_Uncertainty_and_Sensitivity_Analyses.html) and [Tutorial 11, Dynamic Simulation](https://qsdsan.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorials/11_Dynamic_Simulation.html).
